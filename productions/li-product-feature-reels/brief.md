## What this video is for

A slate, not one video. Each reel gives a LinkedIn scroller one product or one feature in one pass: enough shape to recognize the problem as theirs and the thing on screen as real, so they click through to the site or take Jon's call. These are the supplemental layer around the sizzle series, which carries the argument; these carry the product. Per Amendment 2, every reel is a teaser: it shows the shape, never attempts the personalized wow, and gets the meeting rather than replacing it.

## Where it serves

Top-of-funnel, launch window and after. The sizzle series (productions/linkedin-sizzle-series/) owns the Monday/Thursday organic calendar; this slate posts Wednesdays so the two never share a day. Distribution, ratified with the brief (Becky, 2026-08-11): every reel posts to LinkedIn; the paid test runs the 15-second cutdowns (gated on the telemetry experiment and the budget ceiling decision due Aug 21); the 30-second parents also post to YouTube. The schedule lives in `rollout-plan.md`, sequenced so no reel posts in the same window as the sizzle reel covering the same territory. Anchored by `../bemo-os/docs/initiatives/ga-launch/00-overview.md` Amendment 2 and the per-product rows of `../bemo-os/docs/internal/initiatives/ga-message-map.md` 3.2.

## Who watches it, and in what context

A cold or warm nonprofit ED or development director scrolling LinkedIn, sound off, three seconds of patience. The no-VO ruling makes this literal: the on-screen text is not a fallback for the narration, it is the whole argument. Secondary context: sent one-to-one by Jon or an affiliate as a "here's what Amplify actually is" follow-up after a first conversation.

## What it must say

1. The problem first, even at 15 seconds. Every reel opens on the viewer's pattern, never on the product name, a logo, or a feature (`18-anti-patterns.md`, "Never lead with features"; hard rule 2).
2. The product's lead message from the message map 3.2, verbatim where it appears as text on screen.
3. The brand close: wordmark, "BeMo. Where missions gain momentum." shown (not spoken; there is no VO), bemointel.ai small.

## What it must not say

- No personalized wow (Amendment 2): the reel shows the shape of the product on the Common Table demo org, never a prospect's own world.
- No feature checklists. One reel, one feature, one message. A reel that names two features is two reels.
- Nothing that frames the products as a bundle or suite; "four apps, one product" is the sanctioned category line if the slate ever says it.
- No GA mention, no countdown, no urgency; beta CTA lives in post copy only, per the pre-GA rules the sizzle series already carries.
- No Allocate.
- No Meg Poe material without a dated per-use line for the specific placement.
- No uncleared names: Patti Connolly's Academy line is not in the customer-stories overview or the pre-clearance table and does not appear.
- No AI-leading language ("AI-powered" and kin stay out of the reels; the technology is not the hook).
- Roadmap surfaces are labeled: anything not shipped renders as "Illustrated" with the fictional-organization provenance line, per hard rule 4.

## Format rules for this slate

- 30 seconds primary, 16:9 1920x1080, motion-cut grammar over the series' 30-second upbeat bed, brand-close end card, per the product feature standard.
- The 15-second cutdown is a marked core inside the 30-second script (script tables carry a CUTDOWN column), so one capture and one edit session yield both.
- **VO, settled (Becky, 2026-08-11): the 30-second parents keep the standard muted-first mix with VO; the 15-second cutdowns carry no VO**, captions, on-screen text, and music only. The cutdown is the parent's timeline with the voice bus off, so the word-timestamp motion sync survives.
- App footage sits in the standard framed 16:10 product window; provenance labels on every product visual.

## Success looks like

A viewer who watched one reel can name the one thing that product does and why it is different, in their own words, in the comments or on Jon's calls. Jon and Bill have a per-product asset to send that answers "what is Amplify?" without a meeting. Nothing in the slate requires explaining the whole platform first.
