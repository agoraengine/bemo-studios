# Brief: Foundational Demo Training Video

**Status:** RATIFIED (Becky, 2026-08-13, via the four scope decisions below)
**Owner:** Becky
**Target length:** 11 to 14 minutes (one-minute frame, six beats at real pace, pricing segment, close)
**Opened:** 2026-08-13

## What this video is for

Show Jon what a correctly delivered foundational demo looks and sounds like, on the real product, before and after his live training session with Lee (being scheduled for the week of August 17). The job is "Jon watches a complete, in-doctrine run of the universal demo so Lee's session can be spent on practice and correction, not on first exposure." It is the reference performance of the script at `../bemo-os/docs/people/affiliates/jon-kelley/foundational-demo.md` (v3, August 13): the same beats, the same lines to land, the same click path Jon will use on Common Table.

Named per the August 13 ruling: "101" belongs to Academy's course-naming register, so this production is the foundational demo training video, not "BeMo 101" (fine as spoken shorthand, not as an asset name).

## Where it serves

Sales enablement, internal. Pairs with Lee's live training session and Jon's practice plan (section 12 of the demo script). This is not one of the six core videos and belongs to no launch wave; it is training infrastructure for the demo motion in `../bemo-os/docs/initiatives/ga-launch/00-overview.md` Amendment 2 (sizzle opens the door, the live session walks through it; this video trains the live session).

## Who watches it, and in what context

Jon, alone, at his desk, probably more than once: before the Lee session for first exposure, and after it as the reference he checks his own practice runs against. Secondary viewers: Becky and Lee, checking the run stays in canon as the script revises, and any future salesperson or affiliate who inherits the demo. Never a prospect, ever (see below).

## What it must say

1. The six beats of the universal demo, delivered with the talk track's lines to land verbatim: "Nothing falls on the floor" (Home); "Generic AI memory can only see what's there. BeMo knows what's missing" (Knowledge Base); "You don't have to be the only one who knows how this organization works" (Compass); "Your next grant cycle should start where your last one ended, not from scratch" (FunderStorm); "Every piece of content reflects who you actually are, not who you were three drafts ago" (Amplify); "Four apps. One product. They share one memory" (close). Each traces per the claim table in the demo script.
2. The bridge that opens the demo, close to verbatim from the ratified problem statement: everything your organization knows lives in people's heads and scattered tools; BeMo is where it lives instead.
3. That this is the universal (Common Table) mode, and that on a real call the default is the prospect's own prepared workspace. The video says this in the frame at the top so Jon never mistakes the fallback for the whole motion.
4. If the pricing segment is included (recommended, see open questions): the beta-posture framing exactly as the script carries it. Show the published pricing page; starting today is free in open beta; the grandfather commitment is printed on the page; never improvise terms.

## What it must not say

- **This video never goes to a prospect.** It is the full demo, and sending it would break the demo doctrine twice: pre-call material stays teaser-level, and the meeting is the demo. The frame at the top says this out loud. If a prospect-facing video is ever wanted, that is the sizzle reel, which exists.
- Nothing that presents Common Table as real: the video says on screen and in narration that Common Table is a demonstration organization. All footage is the real app doing real work on fictitious content (hard rules 3 and 4).
- No Allocate, no internal vocabulary in the demo portion (the demo rehearses external phrasing: "we prepared a workspace for you"). The frame at the top, which addresses Jon directly, may name the prepared-workspace motion plainly but still avoids "PAO" so the video never teaches him the internal word in the external cadence.
- No agent add-ons, nothing on pricing beyond the published page and the beta-posture block, per the message map 2.4 and the script's section 7.
- Nothing from the banned list: no em dashes on screen, no AI tells in narration.

## Success looks like

Jon walks into Lee's session already knowing the shape: Lee spends the hour on delivery, recovery paths, and the mock, not on what the beats are. Jon's own practice runs converge on the reference: same order, same lines, same posture. When the demo script revises, this video gets re-cut to match or is retired; a stale reference is worse than none.

## Decisions (Becky, 2026-08-13)

- **Scope: beats plus pricing plus close.** The six demo beats, then the beta-posture pricing segment on the live pricing page, then the universal-mode close. Discovery stays out: no screen to show, and Lee's mock covers it.
- **Pace: real demo pace.** Navigation and pauses shown honestly; the pace itself is training.
- **Voice: Becky's generated voice**, delivering the talk track first person, as on a real call. Her approved voice setup (the ElevenLabs settings proven in the August 10 pacing A/B).
- **Frame: cards with VO.** About one minute of direct address to Jon, with the never-send rule spoken out loud.

## Open items (not blockers to the script; blockers to capture)

- **Compass answer variance.** Beat 3's answer is generated live and differs run to run. The capture takes the best honest take: no retrying until it flatters. If a take comes back thin, the scripted recovery path gets performed and the take is kept as the training moment. Lee verifies the KB load before capture so the answer has its full record to draw on.
- **App access and the capture org.** Capture needs the Common Table capture org loaded and verified (the `productions/common-table-kb-load` loader plus the fact-sheet spot checks), separate from Jon's practice workspace. Capture runs on the Cottage 2 harness (Becky's saved session; Lee is not a capture dependency, per the August 13 correction). A probe pass confirms which surfaces are live before the recording run.
