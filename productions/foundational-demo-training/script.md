## Notes

**Opening:** opens on the job the video does for its viewer (Jon watching a correct run before his session), not on a logo and not on the company. The demo portion then opens exactly as a call does: the ratified problem statement, spoken before any feature is named. Problem first, product second, twice over.

**Pacing:** the timings above assume the app responds at demo speed; S10 and S14 are generation waits and their real durations move everything after them. The clock check below was read against a timer with placeholder waits; the capture locks the true runtime. If the total crosses 14:30, the cut comes from S7 scroll time and the S17 scroll, never from the waits, which are the honest part.

**The two voices:** coach rows (F1 to F4, C2, C3) are Becky talking to Jon. Performance rows are the demo itself. The S11 recovery-path aside stays in performance voice deliberately: it is a line Jon should actually internalize as part of the demo, not commentary about it.

**Music:** bed under F and C rows only, per the brief. Demo runs dry. vH18 chain at assemble time regardless.

**Deviation from standards, logged:** runtime sits far over every published ceiling. Ratified in the brief as an internal-training outlier, same class of exception as Academy lessons. Not a precedent for anything prospect-facing.
