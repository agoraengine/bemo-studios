# Brief: GA Sizzle Reel

**Status:** DRAFT, awaiting ratification by Becky
**Owner:** Becky, with Lee for capture
**Target length:** 60-75 seconds
**Opened:** 2026-07-31

## What this video is for

Open a door. A nonprofit ED or development director who has never heard of BeMo watches 60 seconds and comes away wanting a conversation. It is the asset Jon sends before a meeting, the asset Bill can forward into his network, and the piece that debuts publicly in the owned wave.

It is not a demo. Its entire job is to make the live session worth booking.

## Where it serves

- **Wave 0** of [`ga-launch/00-overview.md`](../../../bemo-os/docs/initiatives/ga-launch/00-overview.md), which lists "sizzle reel script and shot list" as Becky plus Lee, not started. Wave 0's exit criteria is that a stranger handed the press kit, the sizzle reel, and the website describes BeMo correctly.
- **Wave 2**, where it debuts publicly as a post rather than an ad.
- Requested by both Jon and Bill, which is what prompted Amendment 2.

## Who watches it, and in what context

Two audiences, one video.

1. **Cold, muted, mid-scroll on LinkedIn.** Captions carry it. The first three seconds decide everything. This is the harder case and the video is built for it.
2. **Warm, sent by Jon or an affiliate ahead of a call.** They already have a reason to watch. This audience is forgiving, so designing for the cold case costs nothing here.

The ICP is small-to-mid nonprofits. The viewer is competent, overloaded, and has been sold to by software companies before.

## What it must say

Three points, from [`messaging-assets-v1.md`](../../../bemo-os/docs/internal/initiatives/messaging-assets-v1.md).

1. **The work keeps happening, but it does not build.** Processes live in people's heads. Grant narratives get rebuilt every cycle. When someone leaves, the organization starts over. This is a structural problem, not a people problem.
2. **BeMo is where the organization's knowledge lives.** Strategy, funding, communications, and learning on one foundation that remembers.
3. **It is not stateless.** Unlike a general AI tool, session 500 knows your organization. That is the difference the viewer needs to feel, not be told.

Institutional knowledge leads. Coordination language stays in the structural layer and never surfaces as the headline, per the July 29 messaging decisions.

## What it must not say

- **No attempt at the personalized wow.** Amendment 2 is explicit: this video shows the shape of the product and the problem it solves. The wow is the prospect's own organization on screen and that only exists live. Sizzle opens the door, the live session walks through it.
- **No "coordination collapse."** Internal diagnostic language. Becky flagged it on July 28 specifically because it was reading as external copy.
- **No feature tour.** Four products in 60 seconds is a website page, not a video.
- **No roadmap shown as shipped.** Allocate, agents, and anything behind the two-surfaces build stay out unless they are live at capture.
- **No named customer** unless cleared below.
- **No AI-company visual language.** No neural networks, no glowing orbs, no abstract data particles.

## Success looks like

- Jon sends it and books meetings he was not booking before.
- Bill can forward it into the Blackbaud network without adding a paragraph of explanation.
- In Wave 2 comments, strangers describe BeMo in language close to what we intended. That is the scale gate from doc 11, and this video is one of the instruments that measures it.

Not a success metric: view count.
