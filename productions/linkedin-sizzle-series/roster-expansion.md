## Per-reel briefs

### 8. Day One

**For:** the buyer burned by implementations. **Open:** the last software you bought took four months to set up and nobody uses it. **Message:** "Unlike traditional nonprofit software, there's no months-long setup and no rigid workflows: you start with real work on day one" (`messaging-assets-v1.md` section 1, differentiator 2). **Screen:** the 13-document KB load, compressed: drop the documents in, and the organization is present, answering. Recapture on Common Table (the existing Wrenfield footage proves the shot works; the standing universe is Common Table). **Must not:** promise minutes or any specific duration; "day one" is the claim, nothing tighter. **Proof:** the real app doing it.

### 9. It Tells You What's Missing

Backlog promotion. **For:** the ED who suspects the org's record has holes but can't see them. **Open:** you don't know what your organization forgot to write down. **Message:** the KB "also tells you what's missing" (`ga-message-map.md` 3.2, Knowledge Base row); kin to "BeMo makes organizations more legible to themselves" (claim map, full-strength). **Screen:** loaded KB, then the gaps surfaced. **Proof:** MaryEllen O'Donohue, "I love that BeMo can tell you the 'gaps' that exist in your Knowledge Base," blanket approval (July 31). Quote card as written, in her name, never voiced. **Must not:** drift into the generic-AI-memory comparison line ("can only see what's there"), which is cleared for sales and advisor registers, not published copy.

### 10. Where Your Last Cycle Ended

**For:** whoever owns the grant calendar. **Open:** every cycle, the same narrative gets rebuilt from scratch. **Message:** "Your next grant cycle should start where your last one ended, not from scratch" (`ga-message-map.md` 3.2, FunderStorm row); claim map: "BeMo creates a different starting point for every grant cycle." **Screen:** picking a cycle back up in the real app: last year's narrative, budget history, funder notes already there. **Proof:** MaryEllen, verified grant programs she "wouldn't have known to search for," found through chat (blanket). **Must not:** database size, volume, or discovery as the headline; fit over volume is the FunderStorm rule, and continuity is this reel's frame. Reel 2's constraint (grant cycle as one instance of the reconstruction tax) is untouched; this is the product reel, and the two must read as different arguments at script time.

### 11. It Says "I Don't Know"

**For:** anyone whose AI skepticism is really accuracy skepticism. **Open:** the problem with asking AI is that it always answers. **Message:** "BeMo tells you when it doesn't know," publishable behavior claim validated in beta (`ga-message-map.md` 2.3); paired with series claim 3, receipts or silence. **Screen:** two questions in the real app: one answered with sources shown, one met with an honest "that isn't in your knowledge base." **Proof:** the behavior itself, on screen, unstaged: the demo must find a real gap in Common Table's KB, not a scripted refusal. **Must not:** claimed feelings, wellness framing, or any suggestion the honesty is a personality; accuracy is the warmth.

### 12. Sounds Like You

**For:** the person who rewrites every AI draft because it sounds like a vendor. **Open:** the draft was fast; making it sound like you took the rest of the day. **Message:** "Every piece of content reflects who you actually are, not who you were three drafts ago" (`ga-message-map.md` 3.2, Amplify row). **Screen:** the org's voice living in the KB, then a draft that arrives already in it. **Proof:** the product on screen; Jen's blanket-cleared lines are available if a card strengthens the close, but reel 4 is her reel, and using her twice in-series needs a spacing look at script time. **Must not:** "three drafts ago" implies iteration, not a time-savings statistic; no aggregate claims.

### 13. The Grant Narrative Matches the Board Report

**For:** the context switcher. **Open:** three tools, three versions of the truth about the same program. **Message:** "Unlike point tools, nothing lives in a silo: the grant narrative matches the board report, and the donor email sounds like you" (`messaging-assets-v1.md` section 1, differentiator 3); the unifying claim, they share organizational memory (PRODUCT.md, message map 3.1). **Screen:** one fact updated once, then appearing correctly in a grant draft, a board summary, a donor note. **Must not:** name the four products as a feature list; the foundation is the point, the products are where it shows. "Four AI-powered products" stays within its ratified boilerplate use; Allocate never appears.
