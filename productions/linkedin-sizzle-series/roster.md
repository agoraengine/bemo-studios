## The numbering rule

- **Series numbers are publication order.** Reel 1 is what posts first (launch day), reel 2 posts second, and so on. Boards, posts, and people use these numbers.
- **Production codes never change.** Shot IDs (R8-S1), cut codes (8H, vH18), and VO filenames (`hg-r1-*`, `hgh-*`) are frozen capture and audio identifiers from before the renumber. They are internal codes, not names; the ledger maps them.
- A reel's number does not change once assigned, even if its slot moves; the schedule column moves instead.
