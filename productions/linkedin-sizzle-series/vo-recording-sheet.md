# VO Recording Sheet: LinkedIn Sizzle Series, Reel 1 (full session)

One session, all lines, so the whole reel is one voice in one room with one energy. The 60s and 30s cuts share every line except the thesis, which has two versions. (This supersedes the earlier three-line sheet: recording everything fresh in one sitting beats splicing GA-session takes with new ones, and it is what makes the scenes connect.)

**How to run the session:**

1. First, read the whole story straight through, twice, at a natural clip. Lines 1 through 8 in order, a breath between lines, no line numbers. These passes are the flow: each line should sound like it answers the one before it.
2. Then line-by-line takes: say the number ("Line one"), pause a beat, read the line, pause, read it once or twice more with different feels. Best take per line wins, and the straight-through passes are fallback takes too.
3. Drop the raw file in `capture/out/vo/becky/` like the GA session; slicing and leveling happen from the transcription.

**Pace:** brisk but unhurried. The edit owns the silence between lines, so land each line and stop; don't stretch or rush the gaps.
