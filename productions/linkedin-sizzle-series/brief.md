## What this video is for

Five 30-second teasers, each taking a single beat from the GA sizzle reel and giving it room to breathe. They start working before GA: as teaser posts in the founders' ongoing narrative arc, and as 1:1 door-openers Jon sends ahead of a first call. The job pre-GA is the language check: get ICP-fit strangers engaging and describing BeMo in the language we intended, so that by the time Wave 2 spends the same reels as its spine, the message is already proven. None of them is the meeting; all of them earn one.

The series is a mini narrative arc. Watched in order the five reels run feel it, name it, see it differently, hear a peer, discover what exists. Each must also work alone, because LinkedIn delivers them in no order at all.

## Who watches it, and in what context

A nonprofit ED, development director, or consultant scrolling LinkedIn, cold or lightly warmed by the founders' arc content. Assume muted autoplay: every reel must carry its argument in on-screen text alone, with narration as reinforcement for the minority who unmute. Captions on everything, per `../../docs/02-production-standards.md`. Also sent one-at-a-time by Jon and affiliates ahead of a first call.

## What it must say

Series-level claims; each reel leads with exactly one message (one pillar per piece, message house rule).

1. **BeMo is where your organization's knowledge lives.** Verbatim roof, `messaging-assets-v1.md` section 1. Carried by reel 5, echoed as the resolution line elsewhere.
2. **General AI tools are stateless; with BeMo you stop starting over.** Differentiator 1, `messaging-assets-v1.md` ("session 500 knows your organization; session 1 of ChatGPT never will"); claim map: "BeMo creates a different starting point for every grant cycle, board meeting, and strategic planning process." Carried by reels 1 and 2.
3. **It answers from your own record and shows where every piece came from.** Deliverable claim, not capability claim; receipts or silence. Carried by reels 3 and 4 (reel 4 as Jen's attributed story).

## What it must not say

- **No personalized wow.** Amendment 2 binds every reel: show the shape of the product and the problem, never simulate a prospect's own organization. Send the reel to get the meeting, never in place of the meeting.
- **Never lead with AI or features.** Problem first in all five, including reel 5, where the product framing is the resolution, not the open.
- **"Four AI-powered products."** Allocate stays out of external copy until it exits beta (ratified July 29, 2026).
- **No aggregate outcome claims.** No hours-saved statistics, no win rates. Jen's "about an hour instead of 3 days" is an individual, attributed story and stays framed as one.
- **No quote recombination.** Quotes appear on screen, as written, in the speaker's name, never read aloud by the cloned voice. Jen's story is never described as "unsolicited" (provenance correction, July 24).
- **Grant discovery is never the headline.** In reel 2 the grant cycle is one instance of the reconstruction tax, not the frame.
- **"Nothing drops on the floor" stays out** until it is ratified into the message map (flagged in `../ga-sizzle-reel/findings.md`).
- No em dashes, no AI-tell vocabulary, no urgency, no countdowns, no claimed feelings.
