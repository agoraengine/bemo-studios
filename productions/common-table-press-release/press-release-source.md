## How this document is used

The press release gets **built in the app**, in the templates / document editor flow, drafted from the Common Table Knowledge Base. It does not get pasted in from here: the point of the capture is the platform doing real communications work from what the org already knows, and pasting would stage that (hard rule 4 in spirit, and a dull screen besides).

So this file is two things:

1. **The acceptance sheet.** Every fact the in-app draft states must match the trace table below. Where the app's wording differs from the reference draft, the app's wording wins if it passes the voice rules; where a fact differs, the fact sheet wins and the draft gets corrected in the editor (which is itself real, capturable work).
2. **The reference draft.** The shape we steer the interview toward, and the fallback copy if the editor turns out to be free-form with no drafting flow.

Subject choice: the Teen Kitchen graduation already has a release in the KB (`demo-org/common-table/upload/12-press-release-july-2026.md`, dated July 30). The Harvest Supper announcement is the org's natural next piece of work in its August "now," and the KB already holds the event's facts (`upload/11-harvest-supper.md`), so the draft can visibly start from what the org knows.

## Reference draft

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
August 6, 2026
Contact: Marcus Avery, Development and Communications Manager, Common Table Food Pantry

### The Harvest Supper returns October 17: one long table for Corbin Falls

CORBIN FALLS, N.Y. — Common Table Food Pantry will hold its annual Harvest Supper on Saturday, October 17 at the Lincoln Park Pavilion: one seating, long tables, and food from the same suppliers who stock the Mobile Pantry van, cooked in the Pavilion kitchen where Teen Kitchen learns, partly by Teen Kitchen graduates.

Last year 210 neighbors came to dinner and the Supper raised $36,000 after expenses, the pantry's largest fundraising night of the year. This year's goal is $40,000, without raising the ticket price.

"People ask if it is a gala. It is a community dinner," said Deb Fontaine, the board member who chairs the Supper committee. "You sit down next to someone you have never met, and by dessert you know their kids' names. That is the whole design."

The Supper has been Common Table's one fundraising event of the year since 2017, and it does more than raise money: about a third of the pantry's monthly donors first showed up as Supper guests.

"The van runs every week because of this night," said Dana Merritt, Common Table's Executive Director. "If you have ever wanted to see what your neighbors can do when they sit at the same table, this is the night."

Tickets and details are at commontablecf.org.

Common Table Food Pantry is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Corbin Falls, New York. Since 2014 it has served its neighbors through the Mobile Pantry, a weekly grocery route running at three sites across Loomis County, and Teen Kitchen, a semester-length cooking program for local teens. Learn more at commontablecf.org.

## Voice rules the in-app draft must pass

From `upload/13-boilerplate-voice.md`, which the KB holds, so the app may apply some of these itself; whatever it misses gets fixed in the editor on camera:

- **Never "gala."** The committee chair's quote addresses it head-on; nothing else in the release may use the word.
- Plain words: families, groceries, the van, the route. Never "food-insecure populations," "programming," "utilize."
- Numbers with their meaning attached ("$36,000 after expenses" and what it pays for, not "significant impact").
- Not a regional food bank; no county-wide coverage claim ("three sites across Loomis County" is the boilerplate's own phrasing and stays).
- It is the Mobile Pantry; Teen Kitchen is two words, both capitalized.
- BeMo Studios's own rule also applies to what ships on screen: if the model drafts em dashes into the document, editing them out in the editor is part of the work (and a legitimate beat of footage).
