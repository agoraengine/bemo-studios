**The demo-org chip is a doctrine device, not a caption.** It sits small and persistent from D1 through D6 so no frame presents Wrenfield as a customer, per the brief's must-not and the Aug 3 founders decision.

**D7 is the Amendment 2 close.** "The live demo is your organization" hands the meeting-ask to Bill without the video making a pitch; then the brand line, spoken and shown, per the series-wide close standard.

**Consistency rule holds:** every spoken line is carried by burned captions verbatim; on-screen statements (the D7 card) are spoken.

**Speed-ups are honest.** The extraction genuinely took 10 to 30 seconds per document and the full load about six minutes; the timelapse compresses time visibly (fast motion, not hidden cuts). No UI text is edited.
