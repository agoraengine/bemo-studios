**Status:** DRAFT
**Owner:** Becky. Capture runs through the Cottage 2 harness against the live app on Becky's session, the same machinery as the Wrenfield knowledge-base load; Lee is not a dependency.
**Target length:** One continuous take, no cuts, target 2 to 3 minutes real time, pending a timed rehearsal. A 30-second cutdown for feed, per the product-feature standard.
**Opened:** 2026-08-12

## What this video is for

The set piece for GA declaration day: watch an empty organization become a working knowledge base in the real product, in one unedited take. The one day the calendar is allowed to be loud, this is the loud thing. The job is to make a stranger believe the day-one claim (real work starts immediately, no months of setup) without a call, and to give the GA post something people outside BeMo's own graph share on its merits. It is the payoff cut of the "Starting From Zero" theme.

## Where it serves

The GA launch sequence, published on declaration day (P50 the week of September 21, P80 by October 2). Serves the launch content alongside Reel 11 and Lee's launch byline. Top-of-funnel and sales-facing afterward: Jon's post-meeting follow-up send.

## Who watches it, and in what context

Cold LinkedIn traffic on GA day, in feed (the 30-second cutdown), and click-throughs to the full take on YouTube or the site. After GA day: prospects post-meeting, when "how fast could we actually start" is the live question.

## What it must say

1. You start with real work on day one; no months-long setup, no rigid workflows. (Messaging assets, "how it's different" line, ratified.)
2. "BeMo is where your organization's knowledge lives," made literal: the knowledge visibly arrives and stays. (The roof.)
3. Even a fresh organization gets told what's missing. (Gap-honesty pair; the honest beat that keeps the take from reading as a stunt.)

## What it must not say

- No personalized wow (Amendment 2). This is the shape of the product on Common Table's data. The viewer's own organization on screen only exists live; the video says a version of "imagine this with your organization's knowledge" only as invitation to a call, never as a claim.
- No time trickery. One take means one take: a visible elapsed clock, no speed-ups presented as real time. If the honest runtime is longer than the target, the runtime changes, not the honesty. Companion copy never says "in minutes" or "easy" (banned false-simplicity language).
- No roadmap features. Whatever surface is stable on declaration day is what appears; nothing shown as shipped that is not.
- Common Table only. No data implying a real customer.
- Nothing publishes before the declaration; no countdown to it.
