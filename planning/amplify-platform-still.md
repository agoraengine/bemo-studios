## The batch (v5): one claim set, four formats

The narrative-arc principle, applied to Amplify: the customer's words are the story, the reel shows the behavior, the still freezes one frame of the same behavior, and the quote card carries the same words. Nothing in the batch says anything the reel does not say; the formats reinforce rather than accumulate claims.

### What v6 adds (Becky, Aug 14): the KB territory, and mobile fill

Every window now **fills its frame edge to edge**: captures scale to the full column width and crop vertically only, never horizontally, because a text line missing its first letters reads as a broken image. The screens are the frame now; the white ground is margins, not field.

**Type is sized for the feed, verified at feed size** (Aug 15): every frame was proofed at 380px wide, which is what a LinkedIn square actually gets on a phone. Headlines land around 22pt equivalent, support lines around 9pt, both clearly readable. In the app windows, the bold headers read in the feed; paragraph text reads as real-product texture in the feed and becomes readable when tapped open. The one fix that would make app body text feed-readable is a recapture at a narrower viewport (the column reflows narrower, so width-fill lands bigger); queued behind the same login as the other captures, worthwhile for the two Amplify beat stills.

## The one weakness, and the fix

The shelf source is a 1x capture, which is why the wide placement leans on the document (sharp, 2x) over a washed background. A 2x shelf recapture retires it; the session has expired, so:

```

node planning/amplify-platform-frames/capture.mjs   retake the shelf at 2x
node planning/amplify-platform-frames/build.mjs     rebuild every frame
```
