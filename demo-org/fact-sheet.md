## Mission, vision, tagline

- **Mission:** The Wrenfield Alliance helps young people and their families in Kessler County stay fed, stay housed, and stay on track in school.
- **Vision:** A Wrenfield where every kid gets a fair shot and no family falls through alone.
- **Tagline:** Neighbors, not caseloads.

## Programs

### After the Bell (since 2012)
- After-school learning and mentoring, grades 4 to 8
- Three sites: Wrenfield Middle School, Kessler Elementary, and the Alliance's Fourth Street center
- FY2025: 410 students; 15 volunteer mentors
- Regular participants (60+ days) attended school at a 93% rate vs. an 84% district average
- Program director: Jaylen Cross; site coordinator: Lena Vasquez

### The Pantry Route (since 2016)
- Mobile food pantry: two vans, 14 weekly stops across Kessler County
- FY2025: 96,400 meals; 38 volunteer drivers
- Sourcing partner: Kessler County Food Bank (fictional)
- Program manager: Nora Steinfeld

### Home Base (since 2021)
- Family stability: emergency rent and utility assistance plus 12 months of case management
- FY2025: 214 families; average assistance $1,140 per family
- 87% of families were still in stable housing 12 months after exiting the program
- Funded in part by the Kessler County family stability contract ($95,000)
- Program director: Marcus Hale

## Strategic plan 2025 to 2027 (three priorities)

1. **Deepen, not widen.** Hold After the Bell at three sites; invest in program quality and outcome measurement instead of expansion.
2. **Diversify funding.** Grow monthly donors from 180 to 400; keep any single funder below 15% of revenue.
3. **Build the backbone.** Document how the organization works so knowledge survives turnover; improve staff retention; formalize board give/get.
