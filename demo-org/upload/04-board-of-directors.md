# Board of Directors

The Wrenfield Alliance for Youth and Families, Inc. Current as of June 2026. The board has 11 members, meets six times per year, and carries a give-or-get expectation of $2,500 per member.

## Officers

**Alton Reyes, Chair** (board member since 2018)
Senior Vice President, Hartwell Regional Bank. Alton chairs the finance conversation at every meeting and brokered the bank's presenting sponsorship of the Harvest Light Gala. Term ends 2027.

**Dr. Priya Nair, Vice Chair** (since 2020)
Pediatrician, Kessler County Health. Priya connects the Alliance to school nurses and county health programs, and pushed for the outcome measurement priority in the strategic plan. Term ends 2026.

**Sam Kowalski, Treasurer** (since 2019)
CPA and partner, Kowalski & Roth. Sam leads the audit committee and built the operating reserve policy (target: four months; current: 3.2). Term ends 2027.

**Ruth Ellison, Secretary** (since 2015)
Retired principal, Wrenfield Middle School. Ruth helped launch After the Bell's first school site and still interviews every prospective mentor. Term ends 2026.

## Members

**Eleanor Voss** (since 2017). Community volunteer and the Alliance's largest individual donor. Honorary chair of the 2025 Harvest Light Gala.

**David Ochoa** (since 2021). Owner, Ochoa Landscaping. Provides in-kind grounds work at the Fourth Street center and recruits fellow small-business owners as sponsors.

**Tamika Bell** (since 2020). HR Director, Corrigan Plastics. Chairs the 12-member gala committee; secured Corrigan's $10,000 sponsorship.

**Rev. James Whitfield** (since 2011, founding member). Pastor, Wrenfield First Baptist. The last founding member still on the board; hosts two Pantry Route stops in his church parking lot.

**Helen Cho** (since 2022). Attorney, Cho Law Office. Reviews contracts and leases, including the county family stability contract.

**Bert Lindqvist** (since 2016). Retired plant manager. Runs vehicle maintenance for the two Pantry Route vans and jokes that he is the only board member with a favorite wrench.

**Ana Barros** (since 2023). Counselor, Wrenfield High School. The board's youngest member and its bridge to students who age out of After the Bell.
