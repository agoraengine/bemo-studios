# The Wrenfield Alliance: Mission, Vision, and Values

Adopted by the Board of Directors, January 2025.

## Mission

The Wrenfield Alliance helps young people and their families in Kessler County stay fed, stay housed, and stay on track in school.

## Vision

A Wrenfield where every kid gets a fair shot and no family falls through alone.

## Who we are

The Wrenfield Alliance for Youth and Families, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in 2011 by a group of Wrenfield teachers, pastors, and parents who kept meeting the same families in three different waiting rooms. We decided those families deserved one door. Fifteen years later we run three programs, After the Bell, The Pantry Route, and Home Base, with a staff of nine and a volunteer corps of more than 60 neighbors.

We are not a big organization and we do not intend to become one. We would rather do three things well for one county than ten things thinly for a region.

## Values

**Neighbors, not caseloads.** The people we serve are our neighbors. We use their names, we return their calls, and we design programs we would be willing to walk our own families through.

**Show up.** Consistency is the intervention. A mentor who comes every Tuesday matters more than a curriculum. A pantry van that is never late builds more trust than a bigger van that sometimes is.

**Tell the truth about outcomes.** We publish what worked and what did not. When a program number is flat, we say so and say why. Funders and neighbors can handle the truth; they cannot handle discovering we hid it.

**Stay in our lane, know our neighbors' lanes.** We do not duplicate what the food bank, the schools, or the county already do well. We partner, refer, and fill the gaps between them.
