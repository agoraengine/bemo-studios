# Funder relationships

*The Wrenfield Alliance. Internal summary maintained by Corinne Delgado, Director of Development. Updated June 2026.*

## Foundations

### Copperline Foundation
- **What:** $75,000 per year, general operating support. Now in year three.
- **History:** First grant 2024, renewed 2025 and 2026. Renewal decision for 2027 expected October 2026.
- **Contact:** Susan Marchetti, program officer. Prefers a short written update twice a year over site visits; send the annual report with a one-page cover note.
- **Notes:** Copperline moved to general operating support in 2025 after our first-year report. Susan has said the plain reporting on the After the Bell attendance plateau is why they trust our numbers. Do not oversell the renewal; the relationship runs on candor.

### Prairie Light Foundation
- **What:** Declined our 2025 letter of intent ($60,000 requested) for a youth mental health pilot inside Home Base case management.
- **History:** First approach 2025. The decline letter invited a reapplication in spring 2027.
- **Notes:** The feedback was that the pilot design was thin on evaluation. Plan: run a small self-funded pilot semester in spring 2027, reapply with data. Marcus Hale owns the pilot design.

## Government

### State afterschool expansion grant
- **What:** $180,000 per year, three-year award, now in year two. Funds After the Bell's three sites.
- **Requirements:** Annual report due each January; attendance data quarterly. Jaylen Cross and Corinne co-write the report.

### Kessler County family stability contract
- **What:** $95,000 for Home Base assistance and case management.
- **History:** Renewed May 2026, citing the 87% twelve-month housing outcome. Helen Cho reviews the contract terms.
- **Goal:** Convert to a multi-year agreement per the strategic plan.

### City of Wrenfield community grant
- **What:** $55,000 general operating. Annual application each March; low-touch reporting.

## Corporate

### Hartwell Regional Bank
- **What:** Presenting sponsor of the Harvest Light Gala, $25,000, plus employee volunteer days on the Pantry Route.
- **Relationship:** Board chair Alton Reyes is an SVP at the bank; he stays out of the ask itself. Renewal conversation each spring.

### Corrigan Plastics
- **What:** $10,000 gala sponsor. Board member Tamika Bell is the connection.

## Individuals

### Eleanor Voss
- **What:** Our largest individual donor: $50,000 in FY2025. Honorary chair of the 2025 Harvest Light Gala. Board member since 2017.
- **Notes:** Eleanor gives to the mission, not to projects; do not pitch her restricted gifts. She reads every annual report cover to cover and once caught a numbers discrepancy before our auditor did.

### Monthly donors
- 180 monthly donors as of December 2025. Strategic plan target: 400 by end of 2027. Corinne runs a spring and fall upgrade campaign; the newsletter is the primary channel.
