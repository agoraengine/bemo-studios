# Program profile: Home Base

*The Wrenfield Alliance. Updated June 2026.*

## What it is

Home Base is our family stability program. It pairs one-time emergency assistance for rent or utilities (average: $1,140 per family) with twelve months of case management. The money stops an eviction or a shutoff; the year of follow-through is what keeps the family from needing us twice.

## Why it exists

We launched Home Base in 2021 after a decade of watching the same pattern from inside our other programs: a kid starts missing After the Bell, we ask why, and the answer is a housing crisis. Emergency assistance existed in the county but came with no follow-up. The families who got $800 in March were often back in crisis by September. Home Base was designed around the follow-up, not the check.

## The numbers (FY2025)

- 214 families served
- Average assistance: $1,140 per family
- 87% of families who completed the program were still stably housed 12 months after exit
- Case managers carry a maximum of 35 active families each

## Who runs it

Marcus Hale has directed Home Base since its launch in 2021. He came from county emergency services, where he processed the assistance checks that never had follow-up, which is exactly why he took this job. Board member Helen Cho reviews the program's landlord agreements and the county contract.

## How it is funded

The Kessler County family stability contract funds $95,000 of the program; the county renewed it in May 2026, citing the 87% twelve-month housing outcome. The balance comes from general operating support and individual giving. The declined Prairie Light Foundation LOI (2025) proposed adding a youth mental health component to Home Base case management; we plan to reapply in spring 2027 with a sharper design and a semester of pilot data.

## What we are working on

The twelve-month outcome number is strong, and we treat it carefully: it measures families who completed the program, and about one in five families disengages before completing. Reducing that disengagement rate is the 2026 focus. We would rather report an honest 87% with its asterisk than a shinier number without one.
