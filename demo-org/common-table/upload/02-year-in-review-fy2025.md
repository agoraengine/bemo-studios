# Common Table Food Pantry: FY2025 Year in Review

Prepared by staff for the board and shared with funders and donors. Covers January through December 2025.

## The year in one paragraph

2025 was the year the Mobile Pantry became a real program. The Bright Harbor Foundation's two-year, $60,000 award arrived in July and funded the refrigerated van and a three-site weekly schedule. By December the routes were serving families in three neighborhoods every week, and the year closed at 82,400 meals distributed to 340 unique families. Teen Kitchen, launched in January, graduated two cohorts totaling 22 teens. We ended the year modestly in the black.

## Numbers

- Total revenue: $348,000
- Total expenses: $331,000
- Net assets at year end: $158,000
- Operating reserve: $68,000, about 3.3 months of cash expenses
- Meals distributed: 82,400 across all Mobile Pantry sites
- Unique families served: 340
- Teens through Teen Kitchen: 22 (spring cohort of 10, fall cohort of 12)
- Active volunteers: about 45

## Where the money came from

- Individuals: $168,000 (48%)
- Institutional grants: $56,000 (16%)
- Harvest Supper, net: $36,000 (10%)
- In-kind food: $82,000 (24%)
- Other: $6,000 (2%)

Institutional grants in detail: Bright Harbor Foundation $30,000 (year one of the two-year award), City of Corbin Falls community grant $14,000, Loomis County emergency food program $9,000, small grants $3,000.

Individual giving is broad and small by design. The largest single gift on file is $5,000, and we track monthly giving closely; it has been our steadiest line since we started tracking it in 2023.

## What worked

The three-site schedule. Tuesdays at the Riverside Senior Center, Thursdays at Eastgate Elementary, Saturdays in Lincoln Park. Saturday remains the largest stop at roughly 40 families a week, and attendance held steady through the summer months when school meals stop, which is exactly when the need is worst.

## What did not

We still have one van, one paid operations manager, and a Saturday volunteer pool that is stretched. The board paused a second Saturday site in 2024 for exactly this reason, and the constraint has not moved. Growth in 2026 depends on funding a driver, not on demand, which is already there.

## The year ahead

The Bright Harbor relationship enters its first renewal cycle in 2026. Diversifying beyond that single anchor is the board's stated priority through 2027.
