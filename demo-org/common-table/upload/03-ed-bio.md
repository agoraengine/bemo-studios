# Dana Merritt, Executive Director

Dana Merritt has led Common Table Food Pantry since 2021.

Dana grew up in Corbin Falls and spent ten years running restaurant kitchens, the last three as kitchen manager of a sixty-seat dining room, before she ever wrote a grant. She started at Common Table in 2016 as a Thursday volunteer, took over pantry coordination in 2018, and was named Executive Director in 2021 when the founding director retired.

She still cooks at the January volunteer potluck and can pack a produce box faster than anyone on the Saturday route. Under her leadership Common Table added the refrigerated van, the three-site Mobile Pantry schedule, and Teen Kitchen, and grew monthly individual giving into the organization's steadiest revenue line.

Dana lives in Corbin Falls. She says the job is simple to describe and hard to do: know your neighbors, keep the van on time, and tell the truth about the numbers.
