# Common Table Food Pantry: The Harvest Supper

Our one fundraising event of the year. Committee chair: Deb Fontaine (board).

## What it is

A harvest-season community dinner at the Lincoln Park Pavilion, cooked in the same kitchen Teen Kitchen learns in, partly by Teen Kitchen graduates. It is deliberately not a gala: long tables, one seating, food from the same suppliers who stock the van. The event began in 2017.

## 2025 results

- 210 guests
- $52,000 gross, $36,000 net
- Teen Kitchen's fall cohort plated the first course, which nobody who was there has stopped mentioning

## 2026 plan

- Date: October 17, 2026, at the Lincoln Park Pavilion
- Budget: held at $16,000 by board decision (March 2026)
- Net goal: $40,000, without raising the ticket price
- Committee of six, chaired by Deb Fontaine; volunteer crew from the Saturday route

## Why it matters beyond the money

The Supper is where monthly donors become regulars and regulars become volunteers. About a third of current monthly donors first showed up as Supper guests, and it is the one night the whole operation, van crew, teens, board, and neighbors, sits at the same table. Which is the point of the name.
