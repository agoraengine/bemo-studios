## Twelve Corbin Falls teens graduate from Teen Kitchen's summer cohort

CORBIN FALLS, N.Y. — Twelve teenagers graduated tonight from Teen Kitchen, Common Table Food Pantry's semester-length cooking and food-skills program, at a ceremony in the Lincoln Park Pavilion where the cohort cooked and served dinner for their families.

Teen Kitchen is Common Table's second program, launched in January 2025 alongside the Mobile Pantry, the weekly three-site grocery route that distributed 41,600 meals in the first half of 2026. Tonight's graduates are the program's third cohort, bringing the total to 34 teens since launch.

Two of tonight's graduates were named the program's first peer mentors and will return next term to help teach.

"These twelve can feed a room," said Dana Merritt, Common Table's Executive Director. "They planned the menu, costed it, cooked it, and served it. That is not a certificate skill. That is a life skill, and it belongs to them now."

The program is coordinated by Alice Tran with guest instruction from Corbin Falls restaurant owner Omar Haddad, and grew out of a youth nutrition pilot supported by the Meridian Fund in 2023.

Common Table Food Pantry is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Corbin Falls, New York. Since 2014 it has worked to make sure no household in Corbin Falls has to choose between rent and groceries. Enrollment for Teen Kitchen's next cohort opens in September at commontablecf.org.
