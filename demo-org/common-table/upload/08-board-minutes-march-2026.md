# Common Table Food Pantry: Board Meeting Minutes

**March 12, 2026. Held by video call and recorded; transcript on file.**

Present: Priya Menon (chair), Hal Brennan (treasurer), June Castellano (secretary), Walt Emery, Deb Fontaine, Omar Haddad, Lucy Pratt. Staff: Dana Merritt (ED), Marcus Avery. Quorum present.

## 1. Financials (Brennan)

February financials accepted. Reserve stands at $68,000, about 3.3 months of cash expenses, within policy. Monthly giving for February was $15,900, tracking slightly ahead of last year.

## 2. Funding concentration (Menon, Merritt)

Dana presented the institutional revenue picture: the Bright Harbor Foundation is just over half of institutional revenue. Discussion of what a declined renewal would mean for the Mobile Pantry schedule.

**DECISION:** The board adopts a concentration objective: by the end of 2027, no single funder over half of revenue. The objective is named "Diversify funding beyond Bright Harbor" and runs to the end of 2027. Dana stewards it; progress reported quarterly. In motion already: the Meridian Fund letter of inquiry planned for the summer, and monthly individual giving, which has been the steadiest line since tracking began.

## 3. Second Saturday site (Ferreira report, presented by Merritt)

The second Saturday Mobile Pantry site, paused in 2024 when the Saturday volunteer pool was too thin, was revisited.

**DECISION:** Deferred. There is no driver and no route owner, and the board will not open a route it cannot staff reliably. Revisit once a driver is funded. Noted for the record: both times this has come to the board, the blocker was staffing, not funding, and the Bright Harbor renewal is the first money that could cover a driver.

## 4. Harvest Supper budget (Fontaine)

The committee proposed holding this year's event at the Lincoln Park Pavilion again, on October 17.

**DECISION:** Budget held at $16,000 against a net goal of $40,000. Last year netted $36,000 on 210 guests; the committee believes the goal is reachable without raising the ticket price.

## 5. Other business

Teen Kitchen spring session on track; summer cohort enrollment opens April 1. Next meeting April 9.

Minutes recorded by June Castellano, Secretary.
