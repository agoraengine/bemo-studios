# Common Table Food Pantry: Board of Directors

Current as of July 2026. Seven members. The board meets monthly; the next meeting is August 8, 2026.

**Priya Menon, Chair.** Retired hospital administrator. Joined the board in 2019 and became chair in 2023. Priya made the introduction to the Bright Harbor Foundation in 2024 through a contact from her hospital years, the connection that led to the Mobile Pantry award.

**Hal Brennan, Treasurer.** CPA in private practice in Corbin Falls. Keeps the reserve policy honest and presents the monthly financials.

**June Castellano, Secretary.** School nurse at Eastgate Elementary, the Thursday distribution site. Brought the school partnership to the board before she joined it.

**Walt Emery, Member.** Retired grocer. A founding volunteer from the 2014 community meal; still works the Saturday route most weeks.

**Deb Fontaine, Member.** Bank branch manager. Chairs the Harvest Supper committee.

**Omar Haddad, Member.** Restaurant owner. Guest instructor for Teen Kitchen and the reason the pavilion kitchen passed inspection twice.

**Lucy Pratt, Member.** Parent volunteer from the Lincoln Park route. Joined in 2025 to keep a participant's voice in the room.

## Governance notes

Give or get is $1,000, waived case by case so board service never has a price of admission. Terms are three years, renewable once. The board adopted the current funding concentration policy at the March 2026 meeting: by the end of 2027, no single funder over half of revenue.
