# Common Table Food Pantry: Mission, Vision, and Values

Reaffirmed by the Board of Directors, January 2026.

## Mission

Common Table Food Pantry makes sure no household in Corbin Falls has to choose between rent and groceries.

## Vision

A seat at the table for every neighbor.

## Who we are

Common Table Food Pantry is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Corbin Falls, New York. We started in 2014 as a weekly community meal at the Lincoln Park Pavilion, cooked by neighbors for neighbors, and incorporated in 2016 when the line outside got longer than the meal could feed. Today we run two programs, the Mobile Pantry and Teen Kitchen, with a staff of five and about 45 active volunteers, serving families across Corbin Falls and Loomis County.

We are small on purpose. Two programs run well beat five run thin, and our board has put that in writing.

## Values

**A seat, not a line.** Nobody proves they are poor enough to eat here. If you show up, you shop, and you are greeted by name the second week.

**Food people actually want.** Fresh produce, real proteins, ingredients that match the kitchens people cook in. The measure of a distribution is what families take home and use, not what we hand out.

**The van is never late.** Reliability is the service. A route that runs every single Saturday builds more trust than a bigger program that sometimes cancels.

**Say what we know and what we do not.** We count what happens, we report it plainly, and when a number is flat we say so. Our funders get the same numbers our board gets.
