# Program profile: Teen Kitchen

Updated July 2026. Coordinator: Alice Tran.

## What it is

A semester-length cooking and food-skills program for Corbin Falls teens, run in the Lincoln Park Pavilion kitchen. Teens learn to cook real meals on a budget, run a service shift, and plan a week of family groceries. Board member Omar Haddad guest-teaches the knife-skills and service weeks.

## History

Teen Kitchen grew out of a 2023 youth nutrition pilot funded by a $9,000 grant from the Meridian Fund. The pilot proved teens would show up for a kitchen when they would not show up for a classroom, and the full program launched in January 2025.

## Cohorts to date

- Spring 2025: 10 teens
- Fall 2025: 12 teens
- Summer 2026: 12 teens, graduated July 30, 2026. Two graduates were flagged as ready to mentor next term, the first step toward a mentor track.

## What's next

The 2026 letter of inquiry to the Meridian Fund, submitted in July with an $18,000 ask, funds Teen Kitchen's second year and stands up the mentor track. The program officer's earlier note flagged interest in coalition work, and the LOI names the school partnership at Eastgate Elementary. Decision expected in the fall.

Separately, Bright Harbor is opening a youth initiative next year that matches Teen Kitchen closely, and the Amberline Family Foundation, which funds youth programs at $10,000 to $25,000, invited an application in July.
