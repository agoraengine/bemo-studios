# Program profile: The Mobile Pantry

Updated July 2026. Program lead: Ray Ferreira, Pantry Operations Manager.

## What it is

A weekly grocery distribution that goes to where people already are instead of asking them to come to us. Three sites, every week, from one refrigerated van: Tuesdays at the Riverside Senior Center, Thursdays at Eastgate Elementary, Saturdays in Lincoln Park.

## History

The route began in 2022 as a single volunteer-run Saturday distribution out of borrowed vehicles. In July 2025 the Bright Harbor Foundation awarded $60,000 over two years, which funded the refrigerated van and made the three-site weekly schedule possible. The award letter is on file; the relationship is now entering its first renewal cycle.

## Current numbers

- 2026 through Q2 (final counts): 41,600 meals year to date, on pace with last year.
- Saturday in Lincoln Park is the largest stop: roughly 40 families each week.
- FY2025 full year: 82,400 meals, 340 unique families.
- Demand holds steady through the summer months when school meals stop, which is when the route matters most.

## The expansion question

A second Saturday site has been on the table twice. In 2024 the board paused it because the Saturday volunteer pool was already thin. In March 2026 it was revisited and deferred again: no driver, no route owner. Both times the blocker was staffing, not funding. The Bright Harbor renewal is the first money that could cover a dedicated driver, and the question is expected back at the board once the renewal decision lands.

## What the funder asked for

Bright Harbor's program officer, Sarah Chen, asked for outcome data earlier than the reporting deadline required. The year-one program report, due August 15, 2026, leads with the meal counts and site attendance for exactly that reason.
