# Common Table Food Pantry: Strategic Priorities, 2026 to 2027

Adopted by the board March 2026, following the concentration decision recorded in that meeting's minutes.

## 1. Diversify funding beyond Bright Harbor

Runs to the end of 2027. Target: no single funder over half of revenue. Steward: Dana Merritt.

The Bright Harbor Foundation made the Mobile Pantry possible and is just over half of institutional revenue, which is a risk to the exact families the van serves. The work: submit three letters of inquiry to newly aligned funders by October 31 (the Meridian Fund LOI, submitted in July with an $18,000 ask, is the first of the three); grow monthly individual giving, which has risen three straight months through June; and answer the Amberline Family Foundation's July invitation to apply.

## 2. Deepen the two programs, not widen

Hold the Mobile Pantry at three weekly sites until a dedicated driver is funded; the second Saturday site stays deferred per the March decision. Grow Teen Kitchen into a mentor track: two summer 2026 graduates are flagged ready to mentor next term, and the Meridian LOI funds the track's first year.

## 3. Build the volunteer bench

About 45 volunteers keep three distributions running every week, and the Saturday pool is the constraint that has twice paused expansion. 2026 focus: recruit Saturday regulars, qualify a second driver, and keep the January potluck the kind of event people bring friends to.
