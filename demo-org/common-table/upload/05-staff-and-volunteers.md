## Volunteers

About 45 active. The Saturday Lincoln Park route is the largest single crew and the thinnest relative to demand; the 2024 decision to pause a second Saturday site was a volunteer-capacity decision, not a funding one. Recruiting focus for 2026: Saturday regulars and a second qualified driver.

## Operations facts

- One refrigerated van, purchased with the 2025 Bright Harbor award.
- Distribution schedule: Tuesdays at the Riverside Senior Center, Thursdays at Eastgate Elementary, Saturdays in Lincoln Park.
- The pantry itself operates from 41 Cedar Street; Teen Kitchen uses the Lincoln Park Pavilion kitchen.
- Payroll and books: outsourced bookkeeping, reviewed monthly by the treasurer.
