# Common Table Food Pantry: Boilerplate and Voice Guide

For anyone writing on the organization's behalf. Kept by Marcus Avery.

## Boilerplate (use verbatim)

**One line:** Common Table Food Pantry makes sure no household in Corbin Falls has to choose between rent and groceries.

**Short:** Common Table Food Pantry is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Corbin Falls, New York. Since 2014 it has served its neighbors through the Mobile Pantry, a weekly grocery route running at three sites across Loomis County, and Teen Kitchen, a semester-length cooking program for local teens. Learn more at commontablecf.org.

**Tagline:** A seat for every neighbor.

## Voice

Write like a neighbor who happens to run a food pantry, not like an agency.

- **Plain words.** Families, groceries, the van, the route. Never "food-insecure populations," never "programming," never "utilize."
- **People first.** "Families the Saturday route serves," not "beneficiaries." Use names when we have permission, no names when we do not.
- **Numbers with their meaning attached.** "41,600 meals in the first half of the year" beats "significant impact." If the number is flat, say it is flat.
- **No poverty imagery.** We photograph food, kitchens, vans, and volunteers at work. Nobody's hardest day becomes our marketing.
- **The van is the brand.** When in doubt, tell a route story.

## House facts writers get wrong

- It is the Mobile Pantry (a program of Common Table), not "the mobile food bank."
- Teen Kitchen is one program, two words, both capitalized.
- We serve Corbin Falls and Loomis County; we are not a regional food bank and we do not claim county-wide coverage.
- The Harvest Supper is a community dinner. Do not call it a gala.
