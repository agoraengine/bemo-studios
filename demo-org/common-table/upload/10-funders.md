# Common Table Food Pantry: Funder Relationships

Maintained by Marcus Avery. Updated late July 2026.

## Bright Harbor Foundation

Private family foundation in Albany, NY; disburses about $425,000 a year. Our largest institutional funder and the relationship that changed the organization.

- **The award:** $60,000 over two years, July 2025, funding the Mobile Pantry's refrigerated van and three-site weekly schedule. Award letter on file.
- **How it started:** introduced in 2024 through board chair Priya Menon; first letter of inquiry submitted spring 2024.
- **Where it stands:** entering the first renewal cycle. Renewal application submitted July 14, 2026, two days early. The renewal call with the foundation is confirmed for August 5. The year-one program report and donor voices are due August 15; Marcus owes three donor quotes for it.
- **Program officer:** Sarah Chen. She asked for outcome data earlier than the deadline required, so the report leads with the meal counts.
- **Worth watching:** Bright Harbor is opening a youth initiative next year that matches Teen Kitchen closely.

## Meridian Fund

Private foundation in Burlington, VT. Funds child welfare and coalition work.

- **History:** funded our 2023 youth nutrition pilot with $9,000; that pilot became Teen Kitchen.
- **Program officer:** Elena Whitfield, still there. Her last note flagged interest in coalition building.
- **Where it stands:** 2026 letter of inquiry submitted in July with an $18,000 ask, for Teen Kitchen's second year and the mentor track. In review; decision expected in the fall.

## Amberline Family Foundation

Regional family foundation funding youth programs at $10,000 to $25,000. Invited an application in July 2026. Nothing submitted yet; this is one of the three LOIs planned by October 31.

## Public funders

- **City of Corbin Falls:** $14,000 community grant, general operating, renewed annually.
- **Loomis County:** $9,000 emergency food program, tied to distribution counts.

## Policy

Per the March 2026 board decision: by the end of 2027, no single funder over half of revenue. Bright Harbor is currently just over half of institutional revenue, which is why the LOI pipeline exists.
