## Programs

### The Mobile Pantry (routes since 2022) [published: three weekly sites, Lincoln Park Saturdays, ~40 families, 41,600 meals YTD Q2 2026]

- Weekly grocery distribution at three sites: Tuesdays at the Riverside Senior Center, Thursdays at Eastgate Elementary, Saturdays in Lincoln Park (the largest stop, roughly 40 families).
- Began 2022 as one volunteer-run Saturday route. The July 2025 Bright Harbor award ($60,000 over two years) funded the refrigerated van and the three-site weekly schedule.
- 2026 H1 final (Q2 counts): 41,600 meals year to date, on pace with last year, steady through the summer months when school meals stop.
- Second Saturday site: paused 2024 (Saturday volunteer pool too thin), deferred March 2026 (no driver, no route owner), open question now; the Bright Harbor renewal is the first money that could fund the driver. **[published, Compass anchor]**
- Program lead: Ray Ferreira, Pantry Operations Manager.

### Teen Kitchen (since January 2025) [published: semester cooking program, third cohort]

- Semester-length cooking and food-skills program for teens, at the Lincoln Park Pavilion kitchen.
- Cohorts: spring 2025 (10 teens), fall 2025 (12), summer 2026 (12, graduated Jul 30, 2026; 2 flagged ready to mentor next term).
- Grew out of a 2023 youth nutrition pilot funded by the Meridian Fund.
- Coordinator: Alice Tran (part-time).
