## Notes on how we work

- **Corinne Delgado** owns the grant calendar, funder relationships, and the monthly giving program (180 donors and growing). She writes most proposals herself with program staff supplying numbers and stories.
- **Theo Marsh** handles the newsletter (monthly, 4,800 subscribers), social channels, the annual report, and press. Theo is also the de facto photographer at every event.
- **Gwen Okafor** keeps the books, runs payroll, manages the leases on the Fourth Street center, and produces the board financial packet.
- **Sam Petrie** coordinates more than 60 active volunteers: 15 After the Bell mentors, 38 Pantry Route drivers, and the gala committee's event-night crew.
- Program staff own their numbers. Each program reports the same three measures every month: people served, cost per person served, and one outcome indicator chosen per program.

Staff turnover has been low for the sector: two departures in three years. The 2025 to 2027 strategic plan commits to keeping it that way through pay benchmarking and documented roles, so no program depends on what lives only in one person's head.
