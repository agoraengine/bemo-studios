# Strategic Plan 2025 to 2027

*The Wrenfield Alliance for Youth and Families, Inc. Adopted by the board January 2025.*

## Where this plan came from

In fall 2024 the board and staff spent three sessions answering one question: what breaks first if we keep growing the way we grew from 2019 to 2024? The budget nearly doubled in that window and every year added something new. The honest answers: our knowledge lives in individual heads, our funding leans too hard on a few sources, and our programs grew faster than our ability to measure them. This plan is the response. It is deliberately a plan about getting better, not bigger.

## Priority 1: Deepen, not widen

Hold the current program footprint through 2027 and invest in quality.

- After the Bell stays at three sites. Investments: the shared homework protocol, mentor training, and the alumni survey.
- The Pantry Route resolves its capacity question by deepening (second weekly visits) before widening (new stops).
- Home Base focuses on reducing pre-completion disengagement, currently about one in five families.
- Every program reports the same three measures monthly: people served, cost per person served, and one outcome indicator.

**How we will know:** each program's outcome indicator improves or we can explain why it did not, in writing, in the annual report.

## Priority 2: Diversify funding

No single funder should be able to sink us.

- Grow monthly donors from 180 to 400 by end of 2027.
- Keep every single funding source below 15% of total revenue.
- Convert at least one government contract (county family stability) into a multi-year agreement.
- Rework and resubmit the Prairie Light Foundation proposal in spring 2027, with pilot data.

**How we will know:** the revenue concentration table in the board packet, reviewed quarterly by the finance committee.

## Priority 3: Build the backbone

What the organization knows should not depend on who is in the room.

- Document every role, program procedure, and funder relationship so a successor could pick it up cold.
- Benchmark pay annually against the regional nonprofit salary survey; budget for it.
- Formalize the board give-or-get at $2,500 and recruit two members with technology and evaluation backgrounds by 2027.
- Reach a four-month operating reserve (currently 3.2 months).

**How we will know:** a new staff member or board member can get oriented from our own materials in a week, without a week of someone else's meetings.

## What we are saying no to

Naming these keeps us honest: no new program launches through 2027, no expansion outside Kessler County, no fee-for-service ventures, and no chasing funding that would bend the mission to fit the grant.
