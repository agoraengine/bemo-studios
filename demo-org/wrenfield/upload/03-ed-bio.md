# Dana Whitaker, Executive Director

Dana Whitaker (they/them) has led The Wrenfield Alliance since 2019.

Dana came to the Alliance after five years as Director of Development at the Kessler County Food Bank, where they built the food bank's first monthly giving program and doubled its grant revenue. Before fundraising, Dana spent seven years teaching seventh-grade English in Dayton, which is where they learned the lesson that still shapes the Alliance's work: a kid who is hungry or worried about eviction cannot hear anything a teacher says.

Under Dana's leadership the Alliance launched Home Base (2021), its family stability program, grew the annual budget from $760,000 to $1.42 million, and adopted the 2025 to 2027 strategic plan that deliberately caps program expansion in favor of depth and measurement.

Dana serves on the Kessler County Continuum of Care committee and speaks regularly at regional conferences about small-shop fundraising. They live in Wrenfield with their partner and two rescue dogs, and they still keep a seventh-grade vocabulary poster above their desk.
