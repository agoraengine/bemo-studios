# Events: the Harvest Light Gala and the Route Run 5K

*The Wrenfield Alliance. Updated June 2026.*

## Harvest Light Gala

Our signature event, held every October at the Wrenfield Armory since 2014.

### 2025 results
- 340 guests
- $268,000 gross, $164,000 net (our best year yet)
- Presenting sponsor: Hartwell Regional Bank ($25,000)
- Additional sponsors included Corrigan Plastics ($10,000) and eleven local businesses

### How it runs
Board member Tamika Bell chairs the 12-member gala committee, which begins meeting in April. The honorary chair is named each spring: a well-regarded community member who lends their name, hosts the top tables, and opens the evening. Eleanor Voss served as 2025 honorary chair. Committee members typically give or secure sponsorships themselves; the committee collectively accounted for roughly a quarter of 2025 sponsorship revenue.

The program is deliberately short: one welcome, one seven-minute story from a program family (with their enthusiastic consent, and they preview the script), one ask, dessert. No auction since 2022; the paddle raise outperforms it and the room goes home earlier.

- Honorary chair: to be announced (committee shortlist due July)
- Goal: $175,000 net

## Route Run 5K

A spring fundraiser for the Pantry Route, run along the actual route of the Saturday van circuit.

### 2025 results
- 480 registered runners
- $26,000 net
- Organized by Sam Petrie with the volunteer driver corps as course marshals

The Route Run is intentionally low-lift: registration, t-shirts, and local sponsors covering hard costs. Its real value beyond the net is recruitment; a third of our new volunteer drivers each year first show up as runners.
